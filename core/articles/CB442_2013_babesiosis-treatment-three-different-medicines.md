## Abstract-derived summary

The study describes dogs with babesiosis seen in Croatia, where fever, anaemia and haemoglobinuria are given as the commonest signs. Infection with B. canis was identified by finding parasites inside red blood cells on blood smears. In total 226 dogs were treated with one of three drugs: diminazene aceturate in 80, imidocarb dipropionate in 72 and fenamidine (Oxopirvedin) in 74. Blood and urine were examined before treatment and again on day 1 and day 7 afterwards, and the clinical, haematological and urinary results were compared statistically. Signs resolved within a day with diminazene, more slowly with fenamidine and slowest with imidocarb. Reinfection within 30 days was not seen after imidocarb but was seen after the other two drugs. Adverse effects were limited to local tissue inflammation at the injection site.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** descriptive
- **Authors' conclusion:** Clinical recovery was fastest after diminazene, slower after fenamidine and slowest after imidocarb, and only imidocarb was followed by no reinfection within 30 days. No side effects were seen apart from local inflammation at the injection site.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** The article is mainly a drug comparison, and its host-side content is the repeated clinical, blood and urine assessment of 226 infected dogs at baseline, day 1 and day 7. The abstract gives no haematological or urinary values, so the pathophysiological contribution (anaemia and haemoglobinuria as presenting features, and their course after treatment) cannot be judged from it. Differences in recovery speed between drugs are associations within this series, not proof of a drug effect, as allocation is not described.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** abstract only; no haematology or urinalysis values in the abstract; allocation to drug groups and any randomisation not stated; species identified on blood smear only; no uninfected control group reported; follow-up of 30 days for reinfection; setting and recruitment period not stated.
