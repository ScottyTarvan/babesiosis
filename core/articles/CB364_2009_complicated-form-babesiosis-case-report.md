## Abstract-derived summary

This report describes a nine-year-old mixed-breed female dog that arrived comatose at a clinic for internal diseases. Doctors diagnosed severe babesiosis with a generalised systemic inflammatory response and failure of several organs. The dog received antibabesial drugs, broad-spectrum antibiotics, a combination of crystalloid and colloid fluids, and a whole blood transfusion. A few hours after admission, overt disseminated intravascular coagulopathy appeared, and the dog died twelve hours after arrival. The post-mortem examination found generalised jaundice, haemorrhagic pulmonary oedema, degenerative liver changes, infarcts in several parenchymal organs, bleeding on the heart and elsewhere, focal necrosis of the cerebrum, acute tubular necrosis, and disseminated intravascular coagulopathy. It is a single clinical case, and it gives no laboratory values or species identification.
