## Abstract-derived summary

Canine Babesia canis infections have risen sharply in Falcon State, Venezuela, but no data on the pathogenesis or molecular characteristics of Venezuelan piroplasms were available. The authors inoculated four dogs, two of them splenectomised, with 1 x 10^6 infected red blood cells and recorded parasitaemia, packed cell volume and body temperature each day. Parasites became detectable after 2 to 6 days. All four dogs developed a mild-type disease, although the authors called the symptoms moderate; the main findings were low parasitaemia, a short febrile period and slight anaemia. Imidocarb dipropionate was judged the treatment of choice, since it cleared parasites from the blood, as shown by PCR. The partial small-subunit rRNA sequence of the isolate was 100% identical to that of a Brazilian B. canis vogeli isolate, and the authors judged the mild clinical course to match that subspecies.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** descriptive (experimental infection, 4 dogs)
- **Authors' conclusion:** The authors conclude that the isolate is B. canis vogeli, citing 100% partial 18S rRNA identity with a Brazilian isolate and the mild disease in the inoculated dogs. They also report that imidocarb dipropionate cleared parasites from the blood, as shown by PCR.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** The study gives a daily record of parasitaemia, packed cell volume and temperature after a defined inoculum, which describes a mild illness with low parasitaemia, a short fever and slight anaemia for this isolate in four dogs. With four dogs, two of them splenectomised, the abstract does not allow comparison of the two groups or inference about severity in natural infection. The anaemia was described as slight, and the abstract reports no other organ-level host data.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** very small sample (4 dogs); the two splenectomised dogs are not reported separately in the abstract; single isolate; no uninfected control group mentioned in the abstract; no quantitative results for parasitaemia, packed cell volume or temperature in the abstract; imidocarb treatment is reported briefly, with no dosing detail; full text not retrieved.
