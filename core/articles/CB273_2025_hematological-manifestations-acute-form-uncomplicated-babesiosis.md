## Abstract-derived summary

Fifty-six dogs with babesiosis, seen at a Lviv veterinary university clinical centre and three private practices between September 2023 and January 2025, had their blood counts described. Anaemia was found in 33 (58.9%) dogs by red cell count and 35 (62.5%) by haemoglobin below 130 g/l; it was normocytic and mostly normochromic, with severe anaemia in 3. Thrombocytopenia affected 55 (98.2%) dogs, graded severe in 17 (30.9%). Leukopenia occurred in 24 (42.9%) and leukocytosis in 14 (25%); lymphopenia in 27 (48.2%) and eosinopenia in 29 (51.8%). Bicytopenia or pancytopenia was present in 42 dogs (75%). The authors conclude that thrombocytopenia, anaemia and leukopenia dominated, commonly together.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** descriptive
- **Authors' conclusion:** Thrombocytopenia, anaemia and leukopenia were the principal haematological findings, frequently combining into bicytopenia or pancytopenia.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** A clinicopathological profile of uncomplicated acute canine babesiosis supporting multilineage cytopenia as the phenotype observed; near-universal thrombocytopenia is consistent with platelet consumption or sequestration but the study cannot distinguish mechanisms. Normocytic anaemia at presentation does not by itself indicate regeneration status, and reticulocyte data are not in the abstract. Anaemia grading text in the abstract is internally unclear: the mild and moderate anaemia counts are given as one entry, 15 (45.45%) of 33, and the severe figure is printed as 9,10% where 3 of 33 is 9.09%.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** species and diagnostic method not stated in abstract; no control group; co-infections not stated as excluded; single time point; heterogeneous clinics and analysers; no outcome data; reference intervals not described.
