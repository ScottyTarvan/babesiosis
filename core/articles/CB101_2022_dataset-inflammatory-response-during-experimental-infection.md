## Abstract-derived summary

This data article accompanies an experimental canine B. rossi model intended to follow systemic changes and test how inoculum size and treatment influence inflammation and disease course. Six healthy male Beagles were used: one splenectomised dog raised the inoculum, three dogs received a high dose and two a low dose. Clinical examination, complete blood count and C-reactive protein were recorded daily, and stored plasma was assayed for cytokines with a canine multiplex bead panel. Treatment was given once preset experimental or humane endpoints were reached. The article summarises every data point gathered over the eight-day infection period. No inferential results are reported in the abstract.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** mechanistic (experimental)
- **Authors' conclusion:** The article presents the collected data as a resource describing inflammatory and clinical progression during experimental infection and treatment; no analytic conclusion is stated in the abstract.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** Valuable as a reusable per-animal time series (CBC, CRP, cytokines, clinical variables) for B. rossi, the virulent African species; interpretation of inflammatory kinetics belongs to the companion analytic paper. Pathophysiological inference from this data article alone is limited.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** n=5 infected dogs, two dose groups of 3 and 2; no uninfected control group (dogs serve as own baseline); treatment at humane endpoints truncates the time series; males only; data article presents no hypothesis testing.

_Abstract-level appraisal; not a formal risk-of-bias assessment._
