## Abstract-derived summary

This Russian-language report, supplied with an English abstract, describes the clinical signs and red cell morphology of dogs with naturally acquired Babesia canis infection. All dogs were febrile. Haemoglobinuria occurred in 70% of cases, a raised pulse rate in 60%, tachycardia in 50% and loss of appetite in 40%. Breathing disturbances and nervous signs were each noted in 20%, and absence of urine production (anuria) in 10%. Red cells showed variation in size and shape, differences in staining intensity and pattern, and abnormal inclusions. The abstract gives no group size, no species-confirmation method, no laboratory values and no comparison with healthy dogs, so the findings are purely descriptive.
