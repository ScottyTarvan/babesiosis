## Abstract-derived summary

Twelve dogs of 8 to 25 kg, naturally infected with Babesia rossi and anaemic (haematocrit 0.1 to 0.2 L/L), were randomly assigned at the Onderstepoort Veterinary Academic Hospital to receive either Oxyglobin (HB-200) or packed red cells, each at 20 mL/kg over four hours, followed by lactated Ringer's solution. Temperature, arterial and mixed venous blood gases, blood pressure, habitus and appetite were followed for 72 hours. On presentation the dogs had a hypoalbuminaemic alkalosis combined with a dilutional acidosis, a fast heart rate with normal pressure, fever, depression and anorexia. The two groups responded much alike, although several parameters differed between them at some time points. Central venous oxygen tension stayed low throughout in both groups. The authors judged Oxyglobin to give broadly comparable improvement.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** descriptive (randomised clinical trial; baseline and post-treatment physiology)
- **Authors' conclusion:** Oxyglobin gave overall improvements similar to packed red cell transfusion in anaemic dogs with babesiosis for blood gas, acid-base and blood pressure. Dogs given packed cells tended to recover habitus and appetite sooner.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** The trial mainly compares two oxygen-carrying treatments, but it also documents the acid-base, haemodynamic and oxygen-delivery state of severely anaemic B. rossi dogs. The consistently subnormal central venous oxygen tension may indicate that tissue oxygen delivery stayed impaired after either treatment, although the abstract reports no tissue oxygen measures. Treatment-group differences were isolated and appear at only some time points.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** only 12 dogs; single centre; no healthy or untreated comparison group; 72-hour follow-up only; many time-point comparisons; survival or long-term outcome not reported in the abstract; abstract-level information only.

_Abstract-level appraisal; not a formal risk-of-bias assessment._
