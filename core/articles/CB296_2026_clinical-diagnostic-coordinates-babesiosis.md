## Abstract-derived summary

This review, published in Romanian with an English abstract, combines published literature with the authors' own clinical observations to describe how canine babesiosis presents and is investigated. It frames the disease as driven mainly by intravascular and extravascular haemolysis, with a spectrum from subclinical infection to life-threatening multi-organ involvement, shaped by parasite, host immunity and environment. Listed signs include lethargy, anorexia, fever, icterus and haemoglobinuria; usual laboratory findings are haemolytic anaemia, thrombocytopenia and altered hepatic and renal variables. Diagnosis is presented as integration of clinical data with blood smear and PCR, while ultrasonography is proposed to reveal splenomegaly and hepatic change that help grade severity. The abstract reports no quantitative results.
