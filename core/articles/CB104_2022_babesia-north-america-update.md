## Abstract-derived summary

This review updates the clinical picture of canine babesiosis in North America. It enumerates five species recognised in the United States (B. conradae, Babesia sp. "coco", B. gibsoni, B. vogeli and B. vulpes), identifies them as apicomplexan parasites living inside red blood cells, and suggests domestic and wild canids may serve as intermediate hosts. The stated aims are to provide practical guidance on clinical manifestations, treatment options and outcomes, and to clarify taxonomy and nomenclature, which have been revised repeatedly since the organisms were first described. Keywords include anaemia and thrombocytopenia. The abstract contains no quantitative data, and the pathophysiology content of the full article could not be assessed.
