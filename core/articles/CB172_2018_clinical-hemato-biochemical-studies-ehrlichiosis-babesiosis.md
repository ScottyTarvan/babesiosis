## Abstract-derived summary

This short report from an Indian veterinary journal describes dogs presented with fever, reduced appetite, ecchymotic skin haemorrhages over the ventral abdomen, nosebleeds and tick burdens, evaluated for the clinical, haematological and biochemical features of tick-borne haemoprotozoan disease. According to the abstract, Ehrlichia and Babesia were the organisms most often encountered. Dogs with ehrlichiosis received oxytetracycline, whereas those with babesiosis received diminazene aceturate; all had supportive care aimed at raising platelet counts, plus liver supplements. The abstract gives no sample size, no number of dogs with each infection, no laboratory values, no statistics, no diagnostic method and no outcomes, so no quantitative findings can be extracted without the full text, which could not be accessed.
