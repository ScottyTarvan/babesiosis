## Abstract-derived summary

Anaemia and thrombocytopenia are the usual haematological changes in canine babesiosis, and Babesia vogeli is the most widely distributed species in South America. The authors sampled 35 dogs from an urban area of Paraguay that showed signs compatible with babesiosis, screened them by nested PCR of the 18S rRNA gene, and sequenced the positives. Four dogs (11%) were positive, with 99-100% identity to the B. vogeli clade, based on comparison with reference sequences. All four were thrombocytopenic; lesser findings were normocytic normochromic anaemia, monocytosis and eosinopenia. The authors present this as the first molecular detection with haematology of B. vogeli infection in dogs from Paraguay. The abstract reports no statistical comparison with uninfected dogs.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** descriptive
- **Authors' conclusion:** B. vogeli is present in dogs in Paraguay, with thrombocytopenia the main haematological abnormality.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** A clinicopathological profile only; it supports thrombocytopenia as a frequent feature of B. vogeli infection but shows no mechanism. Full text indicates PCR-positive and PCR-negative suspect dogs did not differ significantly (platelets 100 vs 245 x10^9/L, p=0.139), so thrombocytopenia is not shown to be specific to infection here. Full text shows anaemia was marked in only two dogs.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** n=4 positives; no healthy controls; co-infections excluded by blood smear only; single time point; no severity or outcome data.

_Abstract-level appraisal; not a formal risk-of-bias assessment._
