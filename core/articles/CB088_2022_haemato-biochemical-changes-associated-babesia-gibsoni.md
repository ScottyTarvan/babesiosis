## Abstract-derived summary

Dogs presenting to two veterinary facilities with inappetence, fever, pallor and lethargy suggestive of babesiosis were screened by blood smear, and 45 confirmed B. gibsoni positive by PCR were enrolled. Each had a physical examination, haematology and serum biochemistry, and findings and clinical outcome were compared with values from normal dogs. Infected dogs showed anaemia of normal cell size and haemoglobin content, mild to moderate thrombocytopenia, variable total leukocyte counts, and differential counts with mild lymphocytosis and granulocytosis. Serum alkaline phosphatase was raised. Numerical values, control group size and statistical results are not given in the abstract, so the magnitude of each change cannot be assessed from it.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** descriptive
- **Authors' conclusion:** The authors report anaemia, thrombocytopenia, leukocyte changes and raised alkaline phosphatase as the haemato-biochemical profile of B. gibsoni infection in dogs.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** A clinicopathological profile of molecularly confirmed B. gibsoni infection, consistent with an erythrocyte-directed anaemia and with platelet consumption or sequestration; regeneration status is not reported. The abstract does not show mechanism, severity stratification or outcome associations; the raised ALP is an isolated hepatic/metabolic signal whose cause is not established.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** no numerical results, controls or statistics in abstract; reference ("normal dog") group origin and size unclear; single region; co-infections, prior treatment and disease duration not addressed in abstract; recruitment period not reported in abstract; regeneration status (reticulocytes) not reported.

_Abstract-level appraisal; not a formal risk-of-bias assessment._
