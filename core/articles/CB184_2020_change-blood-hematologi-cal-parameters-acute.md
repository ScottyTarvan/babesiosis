## Abstract-derived summary

Dogs with babesiosis from Stavropol, Russia, were grouped as acute or chronic cases and their blood counts were set against reference values. In acute disease, erythrocytes were 4.756 x10^12/L (13.5% under reference), haemoglobin 113.4 g/L (5.5% lower), haematocrit 0.325 L/L (12.1% lower) and leukocytes 5.487 x10^9/L (8.5% lower). Platelets fell to 26.67 x10^9/L, 86.6% below reference, with plateletcrit down 90.1%. Chronic cases showed smaller changes: erythrocytes 5.344 x10^12/L (2.8% lower), haemoglobin within reference, haematocrit 5.5% lower, platelets 116.8 x10^9/L (41.6% lower) and plateletcrit 66% lower. Regional epidemiological figures are given as background; group sizes, diagnostic method and statistics are not reported, and no concurrent control group is described.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** descriptive
- **Authors' conclusion:** The abstract states no explicit conclusion; it highlights marked thrombocytopenia as the principal finding.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** Thrombocytopenia dominates and is far deeper in acute than chronic cases, with only mild anaemia (acute Hb about 113 g/L), so platelet loss is disproportionate to red cell loss in this series. It is a haematological severity gradient by disease course, not evidence for mechanism (immune destruction, consumption or sequestration are untested). It cannot be read as prognostic.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** n not reported; no concurrent controls (reference values only); no statistical tests or dispersion type (SD or SEM) stated; definitions of acute and chronic and diagnostic method (smear or PCR) not given; species not confirmed; co-infections and prior treatment unknown; abstract contains unit and translation errors (e.g. plateletcrit in "cells/l"); peer-review standing of this regional journal not verified.
