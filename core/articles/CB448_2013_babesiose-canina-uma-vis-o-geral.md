## Abstract-derived summary

This Portuguese-language review describes canine babesiosis as a tick-borne haemolytic disease attributed to Babesia gibsoni and Babesia canis, the latter divided into the subspecies vogeli, canis and rossi. The authors state that these parasites are carried by different tick vectors and differ in pathogenicity, and that this, together with features of the individual dog, shapes which clinical syndromes appear. They add that differences in form, epidemiology and pathogenesis among the agents contribute to varied geographic distribution and clinicopathological presentation, and make diagnosis harder. The review sets out to cover aetiology, epidemiology, immunology and clinicopathology, together with the diagnostic methods available. The abstract reports no original data, cases or numerical results.
