## Abstract-derived summary

One hundred dogs with clinical suspicion of babesiosis (fever, anaemia, ticks, inappetence, lymphadenopathy, pale or icteric membranes) were tested by PCR, and 24 were positive. Haematology and serum biochemistry of these 24 dogs were compared with a healthy control group. Infected dogs had significantly lower haemoglobin, packed cell volume, erythrocyte count and platelet count, and significantly higher ALT and creatinine. Total protein was lower, but not significantly. The authors present this as the haemato-biochemical profile of naturally acquired canine babesiosis, covering red cell mass, platelets, and hepatic and renal markers. No effect sizes, p-values, species identity or control numbers appear in the abstract.
