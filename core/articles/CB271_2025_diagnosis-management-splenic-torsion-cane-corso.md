## Abstract-derived summary

This single case report describes splenic torsion, a serious acute abdominal emergency in dogs, in a four-year-old female Cane Corso. The dog was referred to the Madras Veterinary College Teaching Hospital after three days of abdominal pain, vomiting, anorexia and lethargy. Examination found an enlarged spleen, and ultrasound showed marked splenomegaly with a lace-like parenchymal appearance, interpreted as suggestive of torsion. A molecular assay detected Babesia at genus level. The animal was stabilised with supportive care including a blood transfusion and treated for babesiosis, then underwent exploratory laparotomy with splenectomy and eventually recovered. The abstract gives no haematology, drug regimen, histopathology or species identity, and it does not state whether the infection contributed to the torsion.
