## Abstract-derived summary

This cross-sectional study from Seropédica, in Rio de Janeiro State, Brazil, examined blood samples from 311 dogs. Real-time PCR followed by RFLP identified Babesia canis vogeli infection in 37 dogs (11.90%). The authors set out to describe the clinical and haematological features of the infected dogs. Clinical parameters were not associated with infection. Haematological assessment showed normocytic normochromic anaemia, haemoglobinaemia and thrombocytopenia, each reported as significant (P<0.05), and the authors describe these as typical of babesiosis due to this subspecies. They conclude that B. c. vogeli infects dogs in the region with considerable frequency and should be regarded as an important pathogen. They suggest it is underdiagnosed, mainly because of coinfections and clinical signs shared with other diseases.
