## Abstract-derived summary

This short article surveys eye disease linked to four tick-borne infections of dogs: ehrlichiosis, anaplasmosis, Lyme borreliosis and babesiosis. The abstract states that the most frequent ocular problems are anterior and posterior uveitis of varying severity, retinal detachment and optic disc neuritis. Conjunctivitis, keratitis or keratoconjunctivitis, and orbital lesions are also mentioned as described presentations. No numerical data, no pathogen-by-pathogen breakdown and no literature-search method appear in the abstract. Babesia-specific eye findings, parasite species and mechanisms are not described there. The publisher page lists only five references, four of them textbooks, which suggests a clinical overview for practitioners rather than a systematic synthesis.
