## Abstract-derived summary

A 9-month-old male Boerboel was brought to a Nigerian university clinic with reduced appetite and brownish-yellow urine. It had pale mucous membranes, fever of 41.2 °C, enlarged prescapular lymph nodes, ocular discharge and Rhipicephalus sanguineus ticks, and had received a multivalent vaccine and rabies vaccine six days before signs began. Blood examination showed intraerythrocytic Babesia and normocytic, normochromic anaemia. The dog died during work-up. Necropsy revealed diffuse pulmonary oedema with interstitial pneumonia, focal cardiac haemorrhage with cellular infiltration, and progressive necrosis of seminiferous tubule epithelium. The authors regard the findings as consistent with complicated babesiosis and highlight testicular degeneration as an unusual systemic complication.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** descriptive
- **Authors' conclusion:** The clinicopathological picture fits complicated babesiosis, including an unusual testicular degeneration not previously described in a Boerboel.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** Illustrates a severe multi-organ phenotype (anaemia, pulmonary oedema/interstitial pneumonia, myocardial haemorrhage) compatible with the severe-babesiosis syndromes, but a single case cannot establish causation. The testicular lesion in particular is hypothesis-generating only. No mechanism is demonstrated.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** One animal; species not identified and no PCR; co-infections (e.g. Ehrlichia, Hepatozoon) not tested; recent vaccination is a confounder; death occurred during the investigation; no follow-up.
