## Abstract-derived summary

This narrative review, from an Indian veterinary college, deals with tick-borne haemoprotozoan infections of dogs. It names babesiosis, hepatozoonosis and trypanosomiasis as the leading members of the group and links their frequency in tropical and subtropical regions to abundant tick vectors. Per the abstract, typical presentations are inappetence, lethargy, anaemia and icterus, with some severe cases fatal, and haematology often shows low platelet counts with raised neutrophils. The paper is structured around epidemiology, pathogenesis and diagnosis, the last covering microscopy, serology and nucleic-acid-based methods. The abstract gives no figures, species-level breakdown or specific mechanisms, so how much of the pathogenesis section concerns Babesia itself cannot be judged from it.
