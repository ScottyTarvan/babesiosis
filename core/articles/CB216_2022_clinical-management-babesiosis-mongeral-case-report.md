## Abstract-derived summary

This single case report describes a six-month-old male mixed-breed pup brought in with a history of inappetence, vomiting and blood in the urine. On examination the animal was febrile (103.5 °F, about 39.7 °C), tachycardic and tachypnoeic, with icteric mucous membranes. Haematology and serum biochemistry were performed, though the abstract gives no values. From the history, clinical findings and laboratory results, the authors diagnosed babesiosis accompanied by immune-mediated haemolytic anaemia. The dog was managed according to this diagnosis and recovered without complications. The abstract does not name the Babesia species, the test used to detect the parasite, the treatment regimen, the laboratory abnormalities or the length of follow-up.

## Critical interpretation

- **Evidence type:** descriptive
- **Authors' conclusion:** The authors state that clinical and laboratory findings confirmed babesiosis with immune-mediated haemolytic anaemia and that treatment led to uneventful recovery.
- **Reviewer interpretation:** Describes an icteric, haematuric syndrome in a juvenile dog that the authors attribute to haemolysis with an immune component. It illustrates a clinical phenotype only; it does not show mechanism, and an immune-mediated component cannot be separated from direct parasite-induced haemolysis on the abstract alone.
- **Key limitations (from available information):** single case; Babesia species and detection method not stated; no abstract-level evidence for immune mediation (for example autoagglutination, spherocytes, Coombs test); no numeric laboratory data; co-infections not addressed; treatment confounding; journal is not MEDLINE-indexed and peer-review status is not stated on the publisher page (not verified, no exclusion on this basis).
