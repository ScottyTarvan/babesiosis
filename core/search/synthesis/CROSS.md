# Cross-cutting themes

Links are relative to the review root; Q1–Q5 refer to the [research questions](#research-questions) below.

**1. Haemolysis, haem, endothelium and AKI.** ERY, END, REN and OXO still describe adjacent parts of one proposed chain, never measured in the same dogs:

- haemolysis ([CB082](articles/CB082_2021_experimental-babesia-rossi-infection-induces-hemolytic.md), CH09 experiment; [CB522](articles/CB522_2016_septic-shock-similarities-differences-postmortem-findings.md), necropsy);
- oxidative change (lipid peroxidation raised in every OXO study that measured it, from [CB353](articles/CB353_2008_erythrocytic-antioxidant-defense-lipid-peroxides-level.md) in 2008);
- endothelial activation ([CB446](articles/CB446_2013_markers-coagulation-activation-endothelial-stimulation-inflammation.md), [CB004](articles/CB004_2017_blood-markers-fibrinolysis-endothelial-activation-babesiosis.md)) and capillary endothelial degeneration in fatal kidneys ([CB341](articles/CB341_2007_histological-ultrastructural-studies-renal-lesions-babesia.md), 2007);
- tubular injury before azotaemia ([CB435](articles/CB435_2012_assessment-renal-dysfunction-using-urinary-markers.md) in 2012, then [CB053](articles/CB053_2020_evaluation-acute-kidney-injury-complicated-or.md), in *B. rossi*; CH01 and [CB024](articles/CB024_2018_glomerular-tubular-kidney-damage-markers-babesiosis.md) in *B. canis*).

The 2006–2016 work weakens a simple haemoglobin-load reading. In CH29 ([CB325](articles/CB325_2006_elevated-serum-urea-creatinine-ratio-babesiosis.md), [CB412](articles/CB412_2011_changes-serum-urea-creatinine-ratio-babesiosis.md)), urea and the urea:creatinine ratio rose while creatinine, cystatin-C and GFR stayed normal, and induced haemoglobinaemia did not reproduce the ratio; in [BG002](articles/BG002_1996_comparative-role-haemoglobinaemia-hypoxia-development-babesial.md) (1996) haemoglobinaemia alone did not cause nephropathy. Urea, creatinine, liver enzymes and MDA did not differ by anaemia status ([CB365](articles/CB365_2009_influence-anaemia-azotaemia-infected-babesia-canis.md), [CB424](articles/CB424_2012_increased-ast-alt-ratio-azotaemic-infected.md), [CB409](articles/CB409_2011_liver-enzyme-activity-infected-babesia-canis.md), [CB394](articles/CB394_2010_malondialdehyde-levels-serum-infected-babesia-canis.md)), though anaemia grade is not haemolysis rate. Warsaw correlations add a haemodynamic co-factor (hypotension, TNF-α, aldosterone; [CB463](articles/CB463_2014_association-between-decreased-blood-pressure-azotaemia.md), [CB473](articles/CB473_2014_increased-concentration-serum-tnf-alpha-correlations.md), [CB485](articles/CB485_2014_hyperaldosteronism-association-hypotension-azotaemia-babesiosis.md)). Cell-free haemoglobin and its scavengers are unmeasured in dogs, so that link rests on malaria ([HU002](articles/HU002_2017_cell-free-hemoglobin-mediated-oxidative-stress.md), [HU010](articles/HU010_2018_intravascular-haemolysis-severe-plasmodium-knowlesi-malaria.md)). Q1 points to co-factors, not one pathway.

**2. Inflammation and coagulation are coupled, but only across papers.** INF, COA, PUL and NEU converge on thromboinflammation in fatal *B. rossi*:

- cytokines: IL-6 and MCP-1 higher in non-survivors ([CB517](articles/CB517_2016_excessive-pro-inflammatory-serum-cytokine-concentrations.md), CH03; [CB037](articles/CB037_2019_disease-severity-blood-cytokine-concentrations-natural.md)), while CRP and SAA did not separate outcome ([CB376](articles/CB376_2009_c-reactive-protein-babesiosis-caused-babesia.md), 2009; CB517);
- consumption: lower factor and protein C activity, longer PT and aPTT and higher D-dimer in non-survivors ([CB438](articles/CB438_2012_mortality-virulent-babesiosis-associated-consumptive-coagulopathy.md), 2012; then [CB145](articles/CB145_2026_thromboinflammation-virulent-babesiosis.md), CH03), with minimal fibrinolysis (CB145);
- tissue fibrin: intra-alveolar fibrin ([CB111](articles/CB111_2023_lung-pathology-natural-babesia-rossi-infection.md), CH04) and cerebral thrombosis ([CB189](articles/CB189_2020_encephalic-babesiosis-clinical-epidemiological-pathological-aspects.md), species unspecified).

CB438 makes the coagulation–death link older and multi-source, but all from Pretoria. Uncomplicated *B. rossi* keeps clot strength, with high fibrinogen ([CB439](articles/CB439_2012_hemostatic-abnormalities-uncomplicated-babesiosis-babesia-rossi.md)), so consumption marks the fatal tail. [CB356](articles/CB356_2008_scintigraphic-evaluation-pulmonary-perfusion-pattern-hospitalised.md) found no large-vessel lung perfusion defects, pointing to the microvasculature. The cytokine and coagulation axes were measured in the same CH03 dogs but never analysed jointly (Q2); the cytokines are public ([S-CB517](datasets.md#s-cb517), [S-CB050](datasets.md#s-cb050)) and the coagulation data request-only (RQ-CB145). NETs and ADAMTS13 are unmeasured in dogs (von Willebrand factor activity only in [CB446](articles/CB446_2013_markers-coagulation-activation-endothelial-stimulation-inflammation.md)); their role is inferred from sepsis and malaria ([HU008](articles/HU008_2019_novel-assay-neutrophil-extracellular-trap-formation.md), [HU009](articles/HU009_2009_severe-plasmodium-falciparum-malaria-associated-circulating.md)).

**3. *B. rossi* versus *B. canis*, *B. gibsoni* and *B. vogeli*.** Species, setting and design remain entangled:

- *B. rossi*: high fibrinogen, consumption in non-survivors, low and falling IL-8, and higher cortisol with lower T4 in severe cases (CH31, [CB040](articles/CB040_2019_clinical-pathological-description-320-cases-naturally.md), [CB105](articles/CB105_2022_serial-changes-concentrations-cortisol-thyroid-hormones.md)). Hypoglycaemia is not insulin-driven, and 16 of 94 dogs were hyperglycaemic ([CB340](articles/CB340_2007_plasma-insulin-concentrations-hypoglycaemic-babesia-canis.md)); hypoglycaemia therefore does not separate *B. rossi* from *B. canis*.
- *B. canis*: thrombin generation with antithrombin III consumption ([CB374](articles/CB374_2009_alterations-some-blood-coagulation-parameters-naturally.md), [CB011](articles/CB011_2017_alteration-haemostatic-parameters-uncomplicated-babesiosis.md), which disagree on DIC); higher IL-8 and KC-like in complicated disease (CH23, one Zagreb source, since [CB510](articles/CB510_2015_serum-levels-chemokines-keratinocyte-chemoattractant-interleukin.md) and [CB511](articles/CB511_2015_razine-kemokina-kc-i-il-8.md) are one study); and an organ-count survival gradient ([CB399](articles/CB399_2010_systemic-inflammatory-response-syndrome-multiple-organ.md), [CB043](articles/CB043_2019_clinical-occurrence-babesiosis-coastal-area-black.md)).
- *B. gibsoni*: immune-mediated (CH17, one source), proteinuric ([CB404](articles/CB404_2011_resolution-proteinuric-nephropathy-associated-babesia-gibsoni.md), [CB018](articles/CB018_2018_azotemia-proteinuria-infected-babesia-gibsoni.md)) and cerebral (CH16, one source) phenotypes, mostly from India.
- *B. vogeli*: mild in intact dogs ([CB021](articles/CB021_2018_first-description-pathogenicity-babesia-vogeli-experimentally.md), [CB339](articles/CB339_2007_parasitological-molecular-characterization-venezuelan-isolate-babesia.md)), severe with splenectomy or co-infection.

The mortality gap has narrowed: 6–12% in *B. rossi* cohorts ([CB338](articles/CB338_2007_endocrine-predictors-mortality-babesiosis-caused-babesia.md), [CB352](articles/CB352_2008_adrenal-response-low-dose-acth-stimulation.md), [CB050](articles/CB050_2020_neutrophil-myeloperoxidase-index-babesiosis-caused-babesia.md), [CB517](articles/CB517_2016_excessive-pro-inflammatory-serum-cytokine-concentrations.md)) against 6–7% in *B. canis* ([CB149](articles/CB149_2026_retrospective-analysis-anamnesis-clinical-signs-therapy.md), [CB100](articles/CB100_2022_clinical-hematologic-findings-babesia-canis-infection.md)). The only within-study species contrast is CH32 in Italy ([CB357](articles/CB357_2008_babesia-canis-canis-babesia-canis-vogeli.md), [CB369](articles/CB369_2009_detection-erythrocyte-binding-igm-igg-flow.md)), where erythrocyte-bound IgG or IgM occurred in 4 of 6 *B. c. vogeli* and 0 of 24 *B. c. canis* dogs. Assay, timing, referral and co-infection confound every other contrast, IL-8 included (Q3).

**4. Most of the work is descriptive; experiments are older than they looked.** Descriptive series and case reports outnumber associational studies in every category. Most experiments are from 2006–2016: [CB370](articles/CB370_2009_systemic-inflammatory-responses-experimentally-infected-babesia.md) (*B. canis*), [CB509](articles/CB509_2015_clinical-haematological-cytokine-acute-phase-protein.md) (*B. gibsoni*), [CB474](articles/CB474_2014_clinico-biochemical-responses-experimental-infection-babesia.md) (*B. canis*) and [CB419](articles/CB419_2011_thrombocytopenia-platelet-activity-experimentally-infected-rangelia.md) (*Rangelia vitalii*); INF counts 11 experimental papers, 8 from round 4. A few older studies manipulate a variable (induced haemoglobinaemia, [CB412](articles/CB412_2011_changes-serum-urea-creatinine-ratio-babesiosis.md); red cell stroma infusion, [CB401](articles/CB401_2010_soluble-parasite-antigens-babesia-canis-do.md); infected-dog serum on reticulocytes in vitro, [CB337](articles/CB337_2007_suppressive-effect-culture-supernatant-erythrocytes-serum.md)). All are small, and none blocks a pathway in infected dogs. Prognosis is no stronger: [CB040](articles/CB040_2019_clinical-pathological-description-320-cases-naturally.md) found no independent predictor, the [CB074](articles/CB074_2021_prognostic-markers-their-discriminant-score-predicting.md) score and [CB516](articles/CB516_2016_prognostic-markers-acute-babesia-canis-infections.md) cut-offs were set on the same dogs, [CB202](articles/CB202_2020_serum-hepcidin-clinical-prognostic-marker-discriminate.md) is confounded by species, and nothing is externally validated. Cohort reuse inflates apparent replication: CH03 holds 6 articles, CH09 5, CH31 and CH16 4 each, and [CB510](articles/CB510_2015_serum-levels-chemokines-keratinocyte-chemoattractant-interleukin.md) and [CB511](articles/CB511_2015_razine-kemokina-kc-i-il-8.md) are one study published twice.

**5. Mechanistic work and data sit in three centres.** Onderstepoort (*B. rossi*), Croatia (mostly Zagreb, *B. canis*) and Warsaw (*B. canis*) hold 16 of the 38 cohorts (6, 5 and 5), which may overlap within a centre unstated.

- Of 15 independent sources with verified individual-level data (21 records), 6 are South African *B. rossi* and 4 Croatian (three dog cohorts, one wolf survey). [DS08](datasets.md#ds08) is probably Zagreb too (country not in the abstract). The rest are single sources: Germany ([S-CB106](datasets.md#s-cb106)), Switzerland ([S-CB516](datasets.md#s-cb516)), the USA ([S-CB042](datasets.md#s-cb042), *B. vulpes*) and Australia ([S-CB013](datasets.md#s-cb013), 7 *B. vogeli* dogs).
- The 2006–2016 work adds one source (S-CB516, laboratory data as a figure only) and one record to an existing one (S-CB517, CH03). The founding endocrine (CH31), coagulation ([CB438](articles/CB438_2012_mortality-virulent-babesiosis-associated-consumptive-coagulopathy.md)) and renal (CH29) studies have no data.
- Neither the Polish work nor the Indian *B. gibsoni* literature that dominates ERY, NEU and CAR has a verified individual-level dataset ([S-CB093](datasets.md#s-cb093) is summary statistics; RQ-CB109, RQ-CB536, RQ-CB134 and RQ-CB300 are request-only).

Reanalysis can address Q2 in part (S-CB517, S-CB050), Q3 ([S-CB017](datasets.md#s-cb017) against S-CB517, with different assays), Q4 ([DS03](datasets.md#ds03)) and Q5 ([DS01](datasets.md#ds01), [DS02](datasets.md#ds02), [DS06](datasets.md#ds06)). Apart from S-CB017, all of these are *B. rossi*.

**6. Measurement artefacts and case definitions cut across categories.** These include:

- spuriously high analyser reticulocyte counts in large-form babesiosis ([CB538](articles/CB538_2016_spurious-reticulocyte-profiles-large-form-babesiosis.md), [CB540](articles/CB540_2016_spurious-reticulocyte-profiles-babesiosis.md); Q4);
- urea rising without a fall in GFR ([CB325](articles/CB325_2006_elevated-serum-urea-creatinine-ratio-babesiosis.md), [CB412](articles/CB412_2011_changes-serum-urea-creatinine-ratio-babesiosis.md), [CB478](articles/CB478_2014_renal-function-integrity-babesiosis.md)), so urea-based azotaemia counts overstate renal disease;
- assays: human cystatin-C (CB325 against [CB501](articles/CB501_2015_assessment-renal-function-using-cystatin-c.md)), non-standard Ukrainian fibrinogen, SOD and catalase normalisation, and haemolysis interfering with AST, bilirubin and coagulation optics;
- detection floors: TNF-α undetectable in 5 of 11 dogs ([CB473](articles/CB473_2014_increased-concentration-serum-tnf-alpha-correlations.md)), insulin mostly below the assay limit ([CB340](articles/CB340_2007_plasma-insulin-concentrations-hypoglycaemic-babesia-canis.md));
- case definitions: CSF erythrocytes that may be contamination (CH16), differing SIRS definitions, species by smear alone;
- sampling after treatment ([CB017](articles/CB017_2018_mcp-1-kc-like-il-8.md) at 6–8 h after imidocarb; [CB499](articles/CB499_2015_lipid-peroxidation-naturally-infected-babesia-canis.md) from day 1), when treatment transiently amplified inflammation in [CB098](articles/CB098_2022_kinetics-inflammatory-response-during-experimental-babesia.md) (Q5).

Pre-analytical and case-definition standards are a prerequisite for every question, not a refinement.

**7. The founding decade set the agenda.** Extending the window to 2006 shows that many 2016–2026 findings repeat older work:

- coagulation: [CB374](articles/CB374_2009_alterations-some-blood-coagulation-parameters-naturally.md) (2009) before [CB011](articles/CB011_2017_alteration-haemostatic-parameters-uncomplicated-babesiosis.md) in *B. canis*; [CB438](articles/CB438_2012_mortality-virulent-babesiosis-associated-consumptive-coagulopathy.md) (2012) before [CB145](articles/CB145_2026_thromboinflammation-virulent-babesiosis.md) in *B. rossi*;
- kidney: [CB435](articles/CB435_2012_assessment-renal-dysfunction-using-urinary-markers.md) (2012) before [CB053](articles/CB053_2020_evaluation-acute-kidney-injury-complicated-or.md); the urea caveat from [CB325](articles/CB325_2006_elevated-serum-urea-creatinine-ratio-babesiosis.md) (2006);
- marrow: [CB418](articles/CB418_2011_serial-haematology-results-transfused-non-transfused.md) (2011) and [CB337](articles/CB337_2007_suppressive-effect-culture-supernatant-erythrocytes-serum.md) (2007) before [CB115](articles/CB115_2023_regenerative-response-naturally-experimentally-infected-babesia.md) (Q4);
- endocrine: [CB338](articles/CB338_2007_endocrine-predictors-mortality-babesiosis-caused-babesia.md) and [CB342](articles/CB342_2007_response-pituitary-adrenal-pituitary-thyroidal-axes.md) (2007) before [CB040](articles/CB040_2019_clinical-pathological-description-320-cases-naturally.md) and [CB105](articles/CB105_2022_serial-changes-concentrations-cortisol-thyroid-hormones.md);
- acute-phase response, lipid peroxidation and organ count: [CB323](articles/CB323_2006_evidence-acute-phase-response-naturally-infected.md) (2006), [CB353](articles/CB353_2008_erythrocytic-antioxidant-defense-lipid-peroxides-level.md) (2008), [CB399](articles/CB399_2010_systemic-inflammatory-response-syndrome-multiple-organ.md) (2010);
- lung: the ARDS lesion in [CB447](articles/CB447_2013_cerebral-babesiosis-acute-respiratory-distress-syndrome.md) (2013, *B. canis*) before [CB111](articles/CB111_2023_lung-pathology-natural-babesia-rossi-infection.md) (2023).

This adds sources, not quality: most round-4 papers are abstract-only (11 of 13 in COA, 23 of 35 in INF), so group sizes and methods are often missing. After 2016 the base shifts towards small Indian *B. gibsoni* series, Ukrainian clinical work and omics; earlier *B. gibsoni* work is mostly experimental or single cases ([CB335](articles/CB335_2007_babesia-gibsoni-detection-during-experimental-infections.md), [CB509](articles/CB509_2015_clinical-haematological-cytokine-acute-phase-protein.md), [CB404](articles/CB404_2011_resolution-proteinuric-nephropathy-associated-babesia-gibsoni.md)).

**8. Reading an automatically categorised map.** The categories are unchecked, final NLP assignments. Three consequences follow:

- Off-topic and low-confidence primaries are common: 3 of 7 PUL primaries do not study the lung, 5 of 14 END primaries do not study vessels or perfusion, 48 of 59 SEV primaries are low confidence, and INF has 18 low-confidence labels. Category size (ERY 96, PUL 7) partly reflects the classifier.
- Key papers sit outside the expected category: [CB145](articles/CB145_2026_thromboinflammation-virulent-babesiosis.md), [CB111](articles/CB111_2023_lung-pathology-natural-babesia-rossi-infection.md) and [CB147](articles/CB147_2026_liver-histopathology-naturally-acquired-babesia-rossi.md) are not END, the Onderstepoort coagulation, cytokine and renal studies are not SEV, and no *B. rossi* article is an NEU primary. Absence from a category is not absence of evidence.
- One paper recurs across categories: [CB516](articles/CB516_2016_prognostic-markers-acute-babesia-canis-infections.md) (15 natural cases) is cited in six syntheses, [CB473](articles/CB473_2014_increased-concentration-serum-tnf-alpha-correlations.md) and [CB497](articles/CB497_2015_cardiac-lesions-babesiosis.md) in at least three. Cross-category agreement can be one study seen several times, and category counts must not be summed.
