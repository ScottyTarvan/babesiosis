### INF — Inflammation, innate/adaptive immunity and immunopathology

**Question this direction asks**

What systemic and tissue host response accompanies canine babesiosis, how does it vary with species, severity, treatment and prior exposure, and does it drive organ dysfunction or merely accompany it? Most studies measure. Only experiments can order events in time, and almost none test causation.

**Evidence base**

- **Assignment.** 91 primary articles (2006–2026), 35 from round 4 (2006–2016). The category is assigned automatically by an NLP classifier, with no human review. 18 assignments are low confidence. Several look off-topic for INF because they mainly report treatment, prevalence or isolate characterisation: [CB336](articles/CB336_2007_possible-treatment-strategy-clinical-factors-estimate.md), [CB339](articles/CB339_2007_parasitological-molecular-characterization-venezuelan-isolate-babesia.md), [CB406](articles/CB406_2011_clinical-haemato-biochemical-therapeutic-studies-babesiosis.md), [CB442](articles/CB442_2013_babesiosis-treatment-three-different-medicines.md), [CB445](articles/CB445_2013_ehrlichiosis-babesiosis-anaplasmosis-hepatozoonosis-st-kitts.md), [CB295](articles/CB295_2026_diagnosis-therapeutic-management-babesia-gibsoni-induced.md). They are kept as assigned.
- **Design.** 9 narrative reviews (none with a reproducible search), 7 case reports, 11 experimental infection or vaccination papers (8 in round 4), 3 necropsy papers, 61 natural-infection studies (4 proteomic). Seven compare survivors with non-survivors: [CB376](articles/CB376_2009_c-reactive-protein-babesiosis-caused-babesia.md), [CB468](articles/CB468_2014_concentrations-infl-ammatory-markers-amino-terminal.md), [CB486](articles/CB486_2014_serum-cortisol-insulin-concentrations-naturally-infected.md), [CB517](articles/CB517_2016_excessive-pro-inflammatory-serum-cytokine-concentrations.md), [CB037](articles/CB037_2019_disease-severity-blood-cytokine-concentrations-natural.md), [CB050](articles/CB050_2020_neutrophil-myeloperoxidase-index-babesiosis-caused-babesia.md), [CB202](articles/CB202_2020_serum-hepcidin-clinical-prognostic-marker-discriminate.md).
- **Species.** *B. canis* 39, *B. rossi* 12, *B. gibsoni* 13, *B. vogeli* 7, *B. vulpes* 1, mixed or unidentified 10, plus 9 reviews. Round 4: 16 *B. canis*, 2 *B. rossi*, 5 *B. gibsoni*, 2 *B. vogeli*, 6 mixed or unidentified, 4 reviews.
- **Abstract-only:** 46 of 91, including 23 of 35 round-4 papers, so many older numbers lack group sizes.
- **Independence.** 81 independent sources, 9 of them reviews. Shared cohorts: CH03 ([CB008](articles/CB008_2017_flow-cytometric-assessment-lymphocyte-immunophenotypes-naturally.md), CB050, CB517), CH04 ([CB054](articles/CB054_2020_pathology-spleen-lethal-babesiosis-caused-babesia.md), [CB147](articles/CB147_2026_liver-histopathology-naturally-acquired-babesia-rossi.md)), CH07 ([CB073](articles/CB073_2021_systemic-inflammatory-response-syndrome-naturally-infected.md), [CB089](articles/CB089_2022_seasonal-differences-intensity-acute-phase-response.md), [CB123](articles/CB123_2024_age-sex-breed-effect-laboratory-parameters.md)), CH09 ([CB082](articles/CB082_2021_experimental-babesia-rossi-infection-induces-hemolytic.md), [CB098](articles/CB098_2022_kinetics-inflammatory-response-during-experimental-babesia.md), [CB101](articles/CB101_2022_dataset-inflammatory-response-during-experimental-infection.md)), CH12 ([CB141](articles/CB141_2025_impact-levels-parasitemia-antibodies-acute-phase.md), [CB149](articles/CB149_2026_retrospective-analysis-anamnesis-clinical-signs-therapy.md)), CH23 ([CB017](articles/CB017_2018_mcp-1-kc-like-il-8.md), [CB510](articles/CB510_2015_serum-levels-chemokines-keratinocyte-chemoattractant-interleukin.md), [CB511](articles/CB511_2015_razine-kemokina-kc-i-il-8.md)). **What has been studied**

*Syndromes.*
- **Acute-phase response (APR).** Seen in natural *B. canis* in Croatia ([CB323](articles/CB323_2006_evidence-acute-phase-response-naturally-infected.md), 2006), Türkiye ([CB058](articles/CB058_2020_serum-amyloid-haptoglobin-ceruloplasmin-albumin-levels.md)), Thailand ([CB081](articles/CB081_2021_characterization-serum-protein-electrophoresis-patterns-c.md)), Slovakia ([CB222](articles/CB222_2022_evaluation-acute-phase-proteins-suffering-various.md)) and Germany ([CB133](articles/CB133_2025_apolipoprotein-1-does-not-appear-be.md)). In CB222 it was the smallest of four diseases, yet SAA and CRP were about 120-fold and 23-fold above controls. It is also present in PCR-positive asymptomatic carriers ([CB057](articles/CB057_2020_evidence-acute-phase-reaction-asymptomatic-naturally.md)).
- **SIRS.** 59% of 54 *B. canis* dogs (CB073); 68% of 332, MODS in 10% ([CB399](articles/CB399_2010_systemic-inflammatory-response-syndrome-multiple-organ.md), secondary).
- **Spleen and macrophages.** White and red pulp merged and macrophages expanded in 9 fatal *B. rossi* cases (CB054, CH04); Kupffer cell and mononuclear change in the liver (CB147). Splenomegaly with rupture in an asymptomatic dog ([CB348](articles/CB348_2008_asymptomatic-babesiosis-cause-splenomegaly-splenectomy.md)); haemophagocytosis after splenectomy in one dog ([CB132](articles/CB132_2025_successful-treatment-severe-babesia-vulpes-infection.md)).
- Brain, lung and thromboinflammation: secondary notes only ([CB189](articles/CB189_2020_encephalic-babesiosis-clinical-epidemiological-pathological-aspects.md), [CB145](articles/CB145_2026_thromboinflammation-virulent-babesiosis.md)).

*Proposed mechanisms (largely untested).*
- **Stereotyped inflammatory programme.** In experimental *B. canis*, dose shifted the timing of CRP, fibrinogen and platelet curves but not their shape, and CRP rose before parasites appeared ([CB370](articles/CB370_2009_systemic-inflammatory-responses-experimentally-infected-babesia.md)). Experimental *B. gibsoni* gave the same CRP-first order, with cytokines later ([CB509](articles/CB509_2015_clinical-haematological-cytokine-acute-phase-protein.md)). In *B. rossi*, inflammation preceded signs and treatment transiently amplified it (CB098).
- **Trigger.** Soluble parasite antigens did not activate kallikrein directly. CRP also rose after infusion of normal red cell stroma, read as a self-molecule contribution ([CB401](articles/CB401_2010_soluble-parasite-antigens-babesia-canis-do.md)).
- **Cytokine-driven injury.** A "cytokine storm" (CB037). TNF-α and IFN-γ rose in *B. vogeli*, confounded by *E. canis* ([CB075](articles/CB075_2021_prevalence-genetic-biochemical-evaluation-immune-response.md), [CB298](articles/CB298_2026_circulating-babesia-sp-ehrlichia-sp-egyptian.md)). IFN-γ/SOCS3 mRNA in severe *B. canis* ([CB109](articles/CB109_2023_haematological-indices-immune-response-profiles-naturally.md)). PGE2 stayed high after therapy in MODS but not SIRS ([CB470](articles/CB470_2014_serum-concentrations-eicosanoids-lipids-naturally-infected.md), abstract).
- **Lymphocyte depression.** CD3+, CD4+, CD8+ and CD21+ were lower than in controls in 10 *B. canis* dogs and rose after imidocarb ([CB490](articles/CB490_2015_changes-selected-subpopulations-lymphocytes-infected-babesia.md)). CD3/CD4 were lowest in complicated *B. rossi* (CB008). Proportions only.
- **Immune-mediated anaemia.** Erythrocyte-bound IgG/IgM in 4 of 6 *B. c. vogeli* and 0 of 24 *B. c. canis* dogs ([CB369](articles/CB369_2009_detection-erythrocyte-binding-igm-igg-flow.md), CH32). Antibodies to *B. gibsoni* Hsp70 cross-reacted with canine Hsp70, which was not on the red cell surface ([CB413](articles/CB413_2011_detection-anti-babesia-gibsoni-heat-shock.md)).
- **Gelatinases.** Low proMMP-2/-9 tracked cytopenias ([CB084](articles/CB084_2021_low-serum-levels-promatrix-metalloproteinase-2.md)).
- **Acquired immunity.** 31% of healthy endemic-area dogs were *B. rossi* seropositive ([CB052](articles/CB052_2020_long-term-follow-up-owned-free.md)). Antibody level correlated inversely with parasitaemia (rho −0.666) and a haematological severity score (−0.652) in CH12 (CB141), though prior exposure is confounded. Young dogs were mostly seronegative with higher parasitaemia (CB123). Soluble-antigen vaccines protected against disease even where *B. canis* parasitaemia stayed unchanged ([CB375](articles/CB375_2009_comparison-babesia-rossi-babesia-canis-isolates.md), review).

*Severity and outcome markers (associations only).*
- **Positive.** IL-6 and MCP-1 were higher in the 12 *B. rossi* non-survivors (CB517); IL-6, MCP-1 and TNF-α in 9 (CB037). MCP-1 and IL-7 separated complicated *B. canis* (AUC 0.906, 0.896, unvalidated; CB017). KC-like and IL-8 were higher in complicated disease (CB510, abstract). Hepcidin gave AUC 0.984, confounded by species (CB202).
- **Negative or mixed.** Admission CRP did not differ (107.5 vs 122.1 mg/l, 57 survivors vs 11 non-survivors; CB376), nor did CRP or SAA in CB517. Procalcitonin separated infected from healthy dogs but not survivors; NT-pCNP did the reverse (CB468, abstract). MPXI exceeded controls in non-survivors but was not compared with survivors (CB050). Cortisol and insulin were reported to predict mortality (CB486, abstract).
- **Proteomics (hypothesis-generating).** [CB476](articles/CB476_2014_identification-serum-biomarkers-naturally-infected-babesia.md) (pooled 2DE), [CB528](articles/CB528_2016_plasma-biomarkers-sirs-mods-associated-babesiosis.md) (five dogs per group; lower complement inhibitors in MODS), [CB112](articles/CB112_2023_serum-proteome-profiling-naturally-acquired-babesia.md), [CB019](articles/CB019_2018_lc-ms-ms-analysis-serum-phosphoproteome.md).

**Convergence and conflict**

- **Agreement.** The APR recurs across countries. Experiments in *B. canis*, *B. gibsoni* and *B. rossi* all show inflammation before or alongside parasitaemia. This extends the earlier *B. rossi*-only (CH09) finding to two more species. In *B. rossi*, CRP failed to separate outcome in two sources (CB376, 2009; CB517) while cytokines did (CB517, CB037); MCP-1 also tracks severity in *B. canis* (CB017). T-cell depression appears in *B. canis* (CB490) and *B. rossi* (CB008), and lymphopenia in 82% of 34 PCR-confirmed dogs ([CB100](articles/CB100_2022_clinical-hematologic-findings-babesia-canis-infection.md)).
- **IL-8.** Lower in infected *B. rossi* and falling with severity (CB517, CB037); higher in complicated *B. canis* (CB510, CB017). Species, assay and timing differ, so the contrast is unproven.
- **Leukogram.** SIRS went with leukopenia (CB073), but complicated disease went with leukocytosis in [CB355](articles/CB355_2008_case-controlled-retrospective-study-causes-implications.md) (35 x 10^9/L or more; species unstated) and CB510. Case definitions probably explain this.
- **Serum proteins.** Alpha-1 globulin was reduced in 54.8% of dogs in [CB405](articles/CB405_2011_abnormalities-serum-proteins-course-babesiosis.md) but alpha globulins were higher in young dogs in CB123. Haptoglobin was low (CB058), unchanged ([CB232](articles/CB232_2023_effect-different-diseases-hepatozoon-canis-distemper.md)) or rose then fell after treatment (CB323), so timing may matter. ApoA-I was higher in SIRS (CB528), unchanged in CB133.
- **Antibody-mediated haemolysis.** Present in *B. c. vogeli* (CB369), unsupported for *B. gibsoni* Hsp70 (CB413). Case reports of immune-mediated anaemia lack confirmatory tests.
- **Mortality.** The earlier contrast of *B. rossi* (11% CB050; 12% CB517) against 1 of 31 *B. canis* (CB017) narrows: 6.7% in 342 German dogs (CB149), 6% in CB100. Virulence differences remain argued ([CB415](articles/CB415_2011_why-southern-african-babesiosis-so-virulent.md)), but referral populations differ.

**Unresolved questions**

- Do IL-6 and MCP-1 at admission predict death in an independent *B. rossi* cohort beyond age and parasitaemia, where CRP and SAA do not?
- Is the IL-8 contrast between *B. canis* and *B. rossi* real? Use one multiplex assay, pre-treatment sampling, neutrophil counts and MPXI in both species.
- Does treatment cause a CRP and cytokine surge in natural infection? CB017 sampled 6–8 h after imidocarb; compare pre-treatment with 6–24 h.
- Is T- and B-cell depression (CB490, CB008) apoptosis, redistribution or loss? It needs absolute counts and function tests.
- Does one SIRS definition at a fixed sampling day reconcile leukopenic SIRS (CB073) with leukocytosis in complicated disease (CB355, CB510)?
- Does admission antibody status predict milder disease independently of age, parasite load and travel history (CB141, CB123)?
- Do erythrocyte-bound IgG/IgM occur in natural *B. rossi* and *B. gibsoni* infection, and track the haemoglobin fall (CB369, CB413)?
