### Research directions in brief

- **Haemolysis–haem–endothelium–kidney axis.** Thrombocytopenia (84–100%) is more consistent than anaemia (26–82%). Tubular injury is detectable before azotaemia in *B. rossi* (from 2012) and *B. canis*, and endothelial activation markers rise. Older Onderstepoort work shows urea rising without a fall in GFR, so urea-based azotaemia overstates renal disease, and organ markers did not differ by anaemia status. A simple haemoglobin-load chain is therefore unlikely; no canine study has measured cell-free haemoglobin, its scavengers and organ-injury markers in the same dogs.
- **Thromboinflammation.** In *B. rossi*, consumptive coagulopathy (from 2012) and IL-6 and MCP-1 excess are each associated with death, while CRP and SAA do not separate outcome. The cytokine and coagulation data come from the same Onderstepoort dogs but were never analysed together. *B. canis* shows thrombin generation with antithrombin consumption; the studies disagree on whether this is DIC.
- **Endocrine and metabolic.** Higher cortisol and lower T4 accompany severity or death in four Onderstepoort *B. rossi* sources (from 2007). Hypoglycaemia is not explained by insulin.
- **Organ syndromes** (AKI, ARDS, cerebral, cardiac, hepatic, pancreatic) are described mostly by small descriptive series, single cases and necropsies. Most experimental infections (*B. canis*, *B. gibsoni*, *B. rossi*) are small, and none blocks a pathway in infected dogs.
- **Species differ.** *B. rossi*, *B. canis*, *B. gibsoni* and *B. vogeli* show distinct phenotypes in different settings, and only one study compares species directly. The mortality gap between *B. rossi* and *B. canis* is narrower than earlier work suggested. Comparable virulence or mechanisms should not be assumed.
- **The founding decade.** Many 2016–2026 findings repeat 2006–2016 work (coagulation, urinary markers, marrow response, endocrine change, ARDS). Most of the older papers can be appraised only from their abstracts.
- **Data.** 21 verified downloadable records with individual dog/sample-level data from 15 independent animal sources, nearly all *B. rossi* or *B. canis* and all small relative to their feature count; see [datasets](datasets.md).
- **Categories are automatic.** Article categories come from the NLP with no human review. Some articles sit outside their expected category, and one study can appear in several categories, so category counts must not be summed.
