Abstract: A study was carried out to determine the prevalence of blood group antigen dog erythrocyte antigen (DEA) 1.1 in mixed breed dogs in rural Chinamhora, Zimbabwe. A survey of DEA 3, 4, 5 and 7 in various breeds is also recommended.

Abstract: BACKGROUND: Canine babesiosis is a tick-borne disease caused by hemoprotozoan parasites of the genus Babesia.
