Journal: Experimental parasitology | First pub: 2022-08-24 | Types: Journal Article | Lang: eng | Src: MED
Abstract: Atovaquone (ATV) has a growth inhibitory effect against Babesia gibsoni. The target site is considered mitochondria, as in the case of Plasmodium spp.; ATV would collapse the mitochondrial membrane potential.
