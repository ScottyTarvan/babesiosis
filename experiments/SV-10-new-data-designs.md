**Read first:** [rules.md](rules.md).

- **Goal.** Three short design memos (each at most 3 pages). **Parallel:** one sub-agent per memo. Depends on nothing.
  1. **Parasite genotype.** Replication of the BrEMA1 link to outcome (CB379, 2009, not replicated) and a genome-wide alternative now that a *B. rossi* assembly exists ([10.1186/s12864-025-11495-z](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-025-11495-z)). Include adjustment for age, days sick and load, and a sample size.
  2. **Host genetics.** A within-breed severe-against-mild design. Candidates to review: red cell traits ([10.1645/GE-2036.1](https://doi.org/10.1645/GE-2036.1)); DEA 1, tested for infection but not for severity (CB143, [10.1186/s12917-025-05117-9](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-025-05117-9)); haptoglobin and cytokine loci. First check PubMed thoroughly for existing canine host-genetic work: the search behind this plan opened only the top records of 48.
  3. **Prospective minimum dataset.** One severity outcome set built from SV-03's definition map; capillary and qPCR load; days sick; antibody status; breed, age, neuter status; a sample before treatment; stored plasma for the mechanisms that SV-06a ranks highest.
- **Outputs.** `out/memo_parasite_genotype.md`, `out/memo_host_genetics.md`, `out/memo_minimum_dataset.md`.
