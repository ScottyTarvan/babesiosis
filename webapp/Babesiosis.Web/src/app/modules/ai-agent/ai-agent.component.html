Articles. The articles loaded to the database: their abstracts, extracted findings and categories, and the papers' own text where it is held.

Experiments. The analyses on the Experiments screen: what each one asked, how it was run, its results and its interpretation. They are this project's own work on small samples, not peer-reviewed findings.

Clinical manual. The pages on the Clinical screen: each syndrome's definition, diagnosis, treatments with doses and evidence levels, and prognosis. The manual is compiled by AI agents and is not peer reviewed: check every dose against the product label before use.

Every claim cites the article, experiment or manual page it comes from, so each answer can be checked against the source.
